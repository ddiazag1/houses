Two side-by-side 3-bedroom cabins (Units 1 + 2): 6 bed / 2 bath, about 1,700 sq ft, about $3,875/mo. Ask whether the two units share a wall or sit next to each other.

Heat: gas in-floor boiler and "Reni" heaters, per the listing (varies by unit). Tenant criteria: income about 2.5× rent, decent credit, clean rental history, references.

Winter without a garage means plugging in block heaters and scraping glass on call nights. The drive to the hospital includes 6.4 miles of the Sterling Highway (state-plowed; the peninsula's main moose and head-on corridor).
